Questions

Frequently asked

What is Trending Today?
It's your own self-published newsroom — write breaking news, opinion, and how-to guides, and they publish instantly with a live trending ticker and ranked homepage.
What goes in "Others"?
Anything that doesn't fit neatly into the other categories — miscellaneous stories, roundups, and one-off pieces still worth publishing.
How is "trending" decided?
Ranked by views — every time an article is opened its view count goes up, so the list reflects what's actually being read right now.
Where is my data stored?
Articles are saved to your personal account storage, so everything you publish is still here the next time you open this page.
